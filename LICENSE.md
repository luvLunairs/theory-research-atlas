## Scope of rights

Facts, mathematical ideas and public-domain material do not become proprietary through indexing. The atlas can grant only rights held by its contributors. Citation is essential scholarly practice, but does not itself grant permission to copy a third party's work.

## Public benefit and permissions

Theory Research Atlas is a voluntary project offered free of charge for public benefit. For material supplied by the founder, the founder makes reasonable efforts, within available time and resources, to identify and credit sources and rights holders, mark applicable third-party terms and seek permission where required. Required permission must be obtained before the relevant use; an original summary and link, or omission of the material, can be used when permission is unavailable.

The project's public-benefit purpose does not replace attribution or permission requirements. This statement does not change the licenses above or restrict the reuse they permit, including commercial reuse.

## Rights concerns

If you believe material in the project infringes your rights, please notify the founder, [@luvLunairs](https://github.com/luvLunairs), using **Issues → New issue → Report a rights concern** in the project repository. The [report form](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/rights-concern.yml) asks for the affected material and an explanation of the concern; supporting links and a requested correction are welcome. Reporting a concern does not require agreeing to license the disputed material or completing research-contribution disclosures.

Issue contents are visible to people with access to the repository and may be public. Do not post confidential documents or personal contact details. If sensitive supporting information is needed, first ask for a private way to provide it without including that information in the issue.

The founder will make reasonable efforts, within available time and resources, to investigate reports and address substantiated concerns. Appropriate action may include correcting attribution or licensing information, seeking permission, replacing material or removing it. Material may be withheld while a concern is assessed; seeking permission alone does not authorize continued use.
