# Scoped review

Review one citation, claim or result. Use this template **or** the form. The [review guide](https://github.com/luvLunairs/theory-research-atlas/blob/main/docs/review-guide.md) offers specialist criteria when needed.

## Material and scope

Link the entry or research record and identify the exact material examined: a saved dated copy, fixed revision, or quoted passage with its page link and reading date. Name the reviewer, role, review date and relationship to the work; name others only with their agreement.

State what you examined and excluded, such as source accuracy, proof correctness, reproducibility, novelty or empirical interpretation.

## Findings

Describe actual checks, exact evidence locations, objections and unresolved questions. Conclude **accepted within scope**, **changes requested**, **rejected with reasons** or **inconclusive**. A planned review remains pending.

If relying on machine verification, link its specification and run, then state what you additionally examined. Automated checks are not human reviews. Acceptance of a citation or special case does not establish the whole result. For corrections, link the earlier review and preserve its findings and objections.

## Credit and AI assistance

Credit sources, contributors and third-party reuse terms. State **No AI assistance**, or provide one [assistance note](https://github.com/luvLunairs/theory-research-atlas/blob/main/docs/ai-contributions.md#one-assistance-note). For citation checks or meaning-preserving presentation alone, briefly name the tool/model if known, its role and actual checks; unknown model details are acceptable. Scientific judgments and their supporting code and evidence need the research details in that note. Link existing notes for this version.

Checking whether a citation matches its source is source curation; judging an argument's correctness is a scientific contribution. A non-recommended or unidentified model's contribution to scientific content or its supporting code and evidence requires [full-text human review](https://github.com/luvLunairs/theory-research-atlas/blob/main/docs/ai-contributions.md#full-text-human-review) of that material and **manual submission of the whole contribution**, even after review. Source curation has no research model restriction. All automation needs authorization and the [final-version recheck](https://github.com/luvLunairs/theory-research-atlas/blob/main/AGENTS.md#required-recheck).
