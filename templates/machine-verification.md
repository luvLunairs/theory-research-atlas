# Machine verification

Propose a checking specification, submit a certificate or report an independent run. Use this template **or** the form; link existing records instead of repeating them. Follow the [machine verification guide](https://github.com/luvLunairs/theory-research-atlas/blob/main/docs/machine-verification.md). Missing approval or an independent rerun leaves **Machine checked** unavailable.

## Target

Give the problem ID, request and kind: exact counterexample, Lean proof, finite construction or finite exhaustion. For a certificate or run, include the attempt ID. Link the approved specification version and human approval, or mark **Pending**.

A new specification needs the exact claim, definitions, quantifiers, assumptions, scope and source passages. Explain why the proposed checker establishes that claim and state its trust assumptions. Changed targets or procedures need renewed approval.

## Certificate and procedure

Link the retained certificate, checker, dependencies and environment at exact versions with SHA-256 hashes. Give the command, inputs, expected output and resource limits; mark missing evidence.

Use exact arithmetic or an approved rigorous certificate. Lean evidence must match the approved target and pass independent proof and transitive axiom checks, without `sorry` or native-evaluation trust extensions. Compilation or a screenshot is insufficient.

## Run, if performed

Link its verification record, or give the operator or authorized service, date, relationship to the contributor, versions checked, retained logs and hashes, outcomes and limits.

Separate self-checks from independent reruns; mark unperformed work pending. **Machine checked** covers only the approved claim and checked version, not human review, novelty, empirical support or a broader solution.

## Credit and AI assistance

Credit sources of every proof and idea, contributors and third-party reuse terms. State **No AI assistance**, or provide one [assistance note](https://github.com/luvLunairs/theory-research-atlas/blob/main/docs/ai-contributions.md#one-assistance-note) for the scientific content and its supporting code and evidence. For presentation-only edits that preserve meaning, briefly name the tool/model if known, its role and actual checks; unknown model details are acceptable. Link existing notes for this version.

A non-recommended or unidentified model's contribution to scientific content or its supporting code and evidence requires [full-text human review](https://github.com/luvLunairs/theory-research-atlas/blob/main/docs/ai-contributions.md#full-text-human-review) of that material and **manual submission of the whole contribution**, even after review. Presentation that preserves meaning has no research model restriction. All automation needs authorization and the [final-version recheck](https://github.com/luvLunairs/theory-research-atlas/blob/main/AGENTS.md#required-recheck); one note may cover assistance, screening and checks.

Machine verification does not waive submission screening. The agent recheck does not replace the independent rerun.
