# Research update

Share an idea, attempt, partial result, failure or candidate answer. Use this template **or** the form. Dated notes or a PDF are welcome; no JSON editing or artifact manifest is needed for an initial contribution.

## Question and outcome

Link the entry and copy the exact claim addressed, with the date read, or link a saved statement. Identify contributors, work dates and any earlier record being revised.

State the assumptions, scope, proposed method and sources, then describe what happened: proposed work, work in progress, unsuccessful attempt, reproduction, partial result or candidate answer. Do not report unperformed work as a result.

## Evidence and checks

Attach or link the dated argument and supporting material. Give actual checks and their limits, or a verification plan for proposed work. For computational work, include the relevant tools, versions, inputs and reproduction instructions.

Separate numerical evidence, proof and support for real-world assumptions. Keep correctness, novelty and significance distinct. Note relevant computing, data, experiment and human assistance needs; label costs as measured, estimated or unknown. Link independent reviews if any. Preserve earlier findings when correcting a record.

Exact certificates and Lean proofs may use the [machine verification route](https://github.com/luvLunairs/theory-research-atlas/blob/main/templates/machine-verification.md); a contributor's own check cannot grant **Machine checked**.

## Credit and AI assistance

Credit sources of every proof and idea, contributors and third-party reuse terms. State **No AI assistance**, or provide one [assistance note](https://github.com/luvLunairs/theory-research-atlas/blob/main/docs/ai-contributions.md#one-assistance-note) for the scientific content and its supporting code and evidence. For meaning-preserving presentation or faithful source curation alone, briefly name the tool/model if known, its role and actual checks; unknown model details are acceptable. Link existing notes for this version.

A non-recommended or unidentified model's contribution to scientific content or its supporting code and evidence requires [full-text human review](https://github.com/luvLunairs/theory-research-atlas/blob/main/docs/ai-contributions.md#full-text-human-review) of that material and **manual submission of the whole contribution**, even after review. Maintenance and source curation have no research model restriction. All automation needs authorization and the [final-version recheck](https://github.com/luvLunairs/theory-research-atlas/blob/main/AGENTS.md#required-recheck); one note may cover assistance, screening and checks.
