# Entry proposal or correction

Use this template **or** a contribution form, not both. For a typo or broken link, a sentence and the credit statement are enough. Unknown details are welcome.

## Suggestion

Identify the entry or page, then describe the change. For a new question, state its assumptions and identify its origin: literature problem, original proposal pending review, or known-result reproduction. A source suggestion needs only its link and relevance.

## Evidence, when relevant

For factual changes, give the source, author, date when known, exact passage or problem number, and when you inspected it. Say what the evidence supports and what remains unchecked. A status change needs checks for later results and equivalent formulations; identify conflicting evidence and changes in assumptions. A special case does not settle the full question.

## Credit and AI assistance

Credit sources, contributors and third-party reuse terms. State **No AI assistance**, or briefly name the tool/model if known, its role and actual checks. Maintenance and faithful source curation have no research model restriction; unknown model details are acceptable.

For original subproblems, research ideas or scientific conclusions, add the [research assistance note](https://github.com/luvLunairs/theory-research-atlas/blob/main/docs/ai-contributions.md#one-assistance-note). A non-recommended or unidentified model's contribution to scientific content or its supporting code and evidence requires full-text human review of that material and **manual submission of the whole contribution**, even after review. Unrelated maintenance may be submitted separately. All automation needs authorization and the [final-version recheck](https://github.com/luvLunairs/theory-research-atlas/blob/main/AGENTS.md#required-recheck); one note may cover assistance and checks.
