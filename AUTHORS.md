The collection brings together work by many researchers. Bibliographic records identify the authors of the underlying literature; inclusion here does not imply their participation in or endorsement of this project. Founding or maintaining the atlas does not confer credit for proposing or solving the indexed problems.

## Recording contributions

Credit follows the work. A contribution should identify its authors and their roles: literature research, curation, problem formulation, mathematical work, software, reproduction or review. Research records carry the names or handles of the people who performed that work, together with its scope and revision. A reviewer is credited only for the review they actually completed.

Contributors may use a professional name, pseudonym or GitHub handle. Add profile or ORCID links only when supplied or confirmed by the contributor. An entry in Git history does not by itself establish scholarly authorship, priority or approval of a result.

To correct attribution or add a contribution, follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Collection citations are provided in [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff); individual research should be cited according to the [reuse and citation guide](docs/reuse-and-citation.md).
