# Theory Research Atlas

Theory Research Atlas is a voluntary project offered free of charge for public benefit. It connects theoretical questions with reliable sources, possible approaches and ways to check progress, helping people and AI combine existing knowledge, transfer methods, search systematically and construct counterexamples.

The **closure of existing theoretical knowledge** is a metaphor for this ambition, not a mathematical boundary or a promise of quick solutions. The collection welcomes bounded investigations and major conjectures, with difficulty and resources assessed separately.

Founded and maintained by [@luvLunairs](https://github.com/luvLunairs), the atlas records work by its founder and other contributors: unsuccessful attempts, partial results, proposed answers and reviews. Its longer-term aspiration is a **human–AI civilization** in which people and AI develop knowledge together.

**New to contributing?** A clearer explanation, a checked citation or a useful source is enough. Open a form from [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md), write in ordinary prose and link your evidence. You can participate entirely in the browser; a maintainer handles the structured records.

**Automated contributors:** start with [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and the entry being changed. **Maintainers:** use [Maintaining the atlas](docs/maintaining.md). Everyone else can start with the form and consult specialist references only as needed.

## The collection

The atlas covers **16 main domains and 113 subjects and subfields**, with **371 source records, 544 candidate problems and 34 detailed cards**. Cards extend existing candidates; they are not extra problems. Entries distinguish literature claims of openness, unresolved status checks, known results and curator proposals. A source-directory link does not mean every problem in that source has been indexed.

**No research attempts or model evaluations have been run by this project yet.**

## Evidence and reuse

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. Credit every proof and idea, including AI assistance, and distinguish proposed work from completed checks. Numerical evidence, formal validity and empirical support have different scopes. Inclusion in the atlas does not certify a solution; each result records its evidence and review status.
