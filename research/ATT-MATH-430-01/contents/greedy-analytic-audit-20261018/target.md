18 October 2026. Scientific work, local draft for full-text human review
and manual submission. The preceding analytic snapshot is immutable.

Target: review the claimed eventual characteristic periodicity of the
strict greedy sequence S(f,g), for all positive integers f<g. Each scanned
integer n>g is selected exactly when it is not the sum of two different
actual earlier selected integers. The task is to find gaps or counterexamples,
repair what can be repaired, and reorganize valid arguments into a continuous
standard proof. Minimal periods, optimal transients and novelty are excluded.

Success requires explicit mathematical justification of parameter boundaries,
seed initialization, all strict-sum exceptions, chronological dependencies,
complete exclusion/coverage, and the infinite continuation in each family.
A sample or an executable success flag is not such a justification.
