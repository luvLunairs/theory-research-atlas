# Two-seed greedy periodicity: an analytic candidate proof

**ATT-MATH-430-01 · 18 October 2026 · Candidate answer awaiting scientific review.**

## Question and outcome

Start with positive integers f<g. Scan the integers above g, selecting an
integer exactly when it is not the sum of two different actual earlier
selected integers. The candidate theorem asserts eventual periodicity of
the characteristic function for every such pair, hence eventual periodicity
of the successive gaps. Equal residues in different blocks may be different
actual summands; a repeated actual integer is forbidden.

Read the [manuscript](contents/greedy-analytic-audit-20261018/manuscript.md)
and its linked mathematical appendices. They form the proposed proof.
Its methods are exact interval/lifetime identities, chronological coverage,
finite disappearance of exceptional entries and a separate causal
supersolution at the midpoint. The latest revision simplifies the strict
pair calculus and compresses two long small-seed arguments. It retains
counterexamples to invalid formula extensions and all necessary boundary
cases. Contributor checks found no unresolved gap in their stated scopes;
that is not independent scientific confirmation.

This is a **two-seed subproblem** of
[Q-MATH-430](../../docs/candidates/Q-MATH-430.md), whose catalogue statement
allows arbitrary finite increasing seeds. No resolution or status change
of that broader problem is requested. Minimal periods, optimal transients,
historical novelty and significance are outside the established outcome.
