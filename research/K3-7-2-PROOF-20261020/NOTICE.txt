The 34-word covering construction is due to Patric R. J. Ostergard and
Heikki O. Hamalainen, New Upper Bounds for Binary/Ternary Mixed Covering
Codes, HUT report A22 (1993), Table III, printed p.22, using the convention
on printed p.16. Its finite mathematical word set is transcribed and
independently verified here. Their paper, prose, scans and figures are not
redistributed.

The lower-bound investigation builds on Dion Gijswijt and Sven Polak,
Semidefinite lower bounds for covering codes, arXiv:2504.01932v2, and
Gijswijt's 2005 thesis. The authors' companion Julia implementation
(CoveringCodes/Julia, commit32b46d8dbdeb49e1743c6ea83760a896bd6ca5eb)
was inspected as prior work. The retained legacy helper
proof/lib/check_lower27_certificate.py credits its q=3 alpha formula to
that implementation. Its upstream MIT notice is preserved unchanged in
LICENSES/Gijswijt-Polak-MIT.txt (copyright 2024-2026 Dion Gijswijt and
Sven Polak). That legacy helper is not executed by the current replay.
The complete Julia implementation is neither bundled nor required.
The raw polynomial/Gram reconstruction and supplied exact certificate
are retained project research artifacts.
