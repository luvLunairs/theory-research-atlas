# Governance

Theory Research Atlas is maintained by [@luvLunairs](https://github.com/luvLunairs). The maintainer is responsible for scope, editorial standards and repository moderation. Contributors retain authorship and rights in their own work.

## Editorial decisions

Assess contributions according to their [work type](CONTRIBUTING.md#requirements-by-work-type): maintenance needs appropriate checks, source curation needs faithful attribution and source checks, and scientific claims need evidence and an honest account of uncertainty. Sensitive maintenance receives targeted assessment within the existing pull request. Useful contributions include corrections, narrower formulations, failed approaches, reproducible computations and partial results. A contribution need not claim a solution to be worth preserving.

The maintainer may merge a contribution, request revisions, seek a specialist review or decline it with a reason. Acceptance into the repository means that the record is suitable for inclusion; the claim's scientific standing is stated separately. Repository consistency checks do not establish mathematical correctness or novelty.

Under the [machine verification policy](docs/machine-verification.md), the maintainer authorizes a fixed specification after human review. An independent pass accepts the certified claim within that scope; merging, novelty and human review remain separate. Changed specifications need renewed approval; defects can lead to withdrawal.

Reviews identify the exact version, assumptions and scope examined. Contributors should disclose conflicts of interest and distinguish their own checks from independent review. Maintainers may curate their own work, but should not present a self-review as independent assessment. Where qualified review is unavailable, the record remains explicitly unreviewed.

The [AI contribution policy](docs/ai-contributions.md) governs assistance disclosure and the additional model eligibility and submission screening requirements for scientific contributions. Those additional requirements do not apply to maintenance or source curation. The maintainer maintains the dated configuration list and recognizes qualifying successors for scientific submissions. Reviewers remain responsible for their findings; AI screening does not establish an independent scientific verdict.

## Disagreements and corrections

Discuss the statement and evidence rather than the person. Support objections with a passage, counterexample, reproducible result or other material that can be checked. A disputed claim retains its objections and review history until the evidence warrants a change. A maintainer's editorial decision does not settle a scientific disagreement.

Corrections should preserve stable IDs and explain changes to assumptions, statements and status. Retractions or superseding results should remain discoverable. Attribution disputes require evidence of the relevant contributions and source dates; uncertain priority should remain uncertain in the record.

People and automated contributors must communicate respectfully, protect confidential information and avoid duplicate or repetitive submissions. Update existing discussions and group related minor fixes; do not flood the project with unexamined output. The maintainer may request smaller batches, close unsupported or repetitive submissions with a reason, remove abusive or unrelated material, or restrict participation. These moderation decisions do not replace scientific review. Raise attribution or conduct concerns through available contribution channels without posting sensitive information publicly.

## Project stewardship

Rights and permission concerns follow the [reporting and correction process](LICENSE.md#rights-concerns). These reports are handled separately from research contributions.

The [project licenses](LICENSE.md) allow reuse, adaptation and redistribution under their applicable terms. Repository moderation and contribution standards do not add restrictions to those licenses.

As the project grows, the maintainer may appoint additional editors with defined responsibilities. Changes to governance or editorial policy should be recorded in the repository and leave earlier authorship and review records intact.
