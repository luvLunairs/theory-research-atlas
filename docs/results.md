## The collection

The atlas contributes an organized research directory: original summaries, subject placement, source comparisons and explicit verification requirements. The underlying questions and published results remain credited to their original authors.

| Resource | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Subject directory](README.md) | 16 main domains and 113 subjects and subfields |
| [Problem index](candidates.md) | 544 identified questions, including 34 full cards |
| [Sources](sources.md) | 371 bibliographic and research-entry records |
| [Literature checks](literature-checks.md) | 376 dated checks, with findings and limitations |

## Research findings

No original research findings have been recorded. Source curation and documentation checks are not counted as solutions to the indexed problems.

[All attempts and reviews](progress.md) include unsuccessful work and known-result reproductions. Every candidate can receive a research record; a full card is optional.

[Machine verification records](verifications.md) track certificate checks separately; they do not add research findings or human review verdicts.

## Contribute

[Propose or correct an entry](../templates/entry-change.md), [report an attempt](../templates/research-update.md), or [review a claim](../templates/review.md). Give the problem ID, exact target, contributor roles, supporting evidence and remaining limits. Follow the [contribution terms](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
