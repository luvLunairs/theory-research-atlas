## The collection

The atlas contributes an organized research directory: original summaries, subject placement, source comparisons and explicit verification requirements. The underlying questions and published results remain credited to their original authors.

| [Sources](sources.md) | 399 bibliographic and research-entry records |
| [Literature checks](literature-checks.md) | 388 dated checks, with findings and limitations |

Preliminary screening describes attributed checks, not correctness confidence or specialist academic acceptance. [Screening policy](review-guide.md#preliminary-screening).

A scoped acceptance does not establish novelty or all aspects of correctness. Corrections and retractions remain visible alongside the affected records.

[All attempts and reviews](progress.md) include unsuccessful work and known-result reproductions. Every candidate can receive a research record; a full card is optional.

[Machine verification records](verifications.md) track certificate checks separately; they do not add research findings or human review verdicts.
