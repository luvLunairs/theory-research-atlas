## Bias and missing evidence

Coverage favors accessible English-language sources and exact discrete certificates. Many continuous, empirical-model and foundational branches have no full card. No new-experiment or public-data task has received a full data/protocol audit. Novelty searches are scoped, not systematic reviews.
