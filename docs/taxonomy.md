## Mathematics

Abstract structures, spaces, quantities and transformations.

[Explore this domain](domains/math.md)

- [Algebra](domains/math.md#math-alg) — Operations, symmetries and algebraic structures.
  - [Commutative and homological algebra](domains/math.md#math-alg-comm) — Ideals, modules and derived algebraic invariants.
  - [Groups and representation theory](domains/math.md#math-alg-rep) — Group structure and linear actions.
- [Number theory and arithmetic geometry](domains/math.md#math-num) — Arithmetic of integers, fields and arithmetic varieties.
  - [Analytic number theory](domains/math.md#math-num-analytic) — Analytic methods for arithmetic quantities.
- [Geometry](domains/math.md#math-geom) — Metric, differential and algebraic structures on spaces.
  - [Algebraic geometry](domains/math.md#math-geom-alg) — Geometric objects defined through algebraic equations.
  - [Differential and symplectic geometry](domains/math.md#math-geom-diff) — Smooth and symplectic structures and their invariants.
- [Topology](domains/math.md#math-top) — Continuity, deformation and global invariants of spaces.
  - [Low-dimensional topology](domains/math.md#math-top-low) — Topology of knots and low-dimensional manifolds.
- [Combinatorics and discrete mathematics](domains/math.md#math-comb) — Finite or discrete configurations and their structure.
  - [Extremal and Ramsey theory](domains/math.md#math-comb-ext) — Thresholds forcing or excluding finite configurations.
  - [Enumerative and algebraic combinatorics](domains/math.md#math-comb-enum) — Counting structures and studying combinatorial algebra.
- [Analysis](domains/math.md#math-anal) — Limits, functions, operators and continuous structures.
  - [Differential equations and variational analysis](domains/math.md#math-anal-pde) — Existence, regularity and structure of ODE/PDE solutions.
  - [Harmonic, real and complex analysis](domains/math.md#math-anal-harm) — Transforms, analytic functions and analytic inequalities.
  - [Functional and operator analysis](domains/math.md#math-anal-func) — Infinite-dimensional spaces and maps between them.
- [Dynamical systems and ergodic theory](domains/math.md#math-dyn) — Long-term behavior under repeated evolution.
- [Optimization and operations research](domains/math.md#math-opt) — Optimal values, algorithms and structural bounds.
- [Numerical analysis and scientific computation](domains/math.md#math-numer) — Approximation schemes with error, stability and complexity guarantees.
