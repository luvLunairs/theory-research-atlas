Determine whether the light neutrino mass eigenstates are Dirac or Majorana, and derive observables that can distinguish these possibilities under specified interactions. In particular, separate statements inferred from a neutrinoless-double-beta-decay mechanism from assumptions about which operator dominates that decay.
