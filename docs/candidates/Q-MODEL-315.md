In the unstratified, zero-net-flux, incompressible Keplerian shearing-box model with fixed box aspect ratio and shear-periodic boundaries, determine the dynamo transition boundary Rm_c(Re), especially whether sustained MRI dynamo action survives as Pm=Rm/Re tends to zero. Keep wall-bounded and stratified geometries separate.
