If a smooth strictly convex planar billiard has an inner annular neighborhood of its boundary foliated by closed caustics, must the boundary be an ellipse? A caustic's tangent lines reflect into tangent lines of the same curve.

**Origin:** Literature-origin question. **Curator-designed scope:** No.

**Current status:** Source reports open. **Last checked:** 2026-10-14.

**Status basis:** The 2026 journal abstract explicitly says the caustic-integrability conjecture remains open, even for algebraic ovals. The polynomial-first-integral theorem has additional assumptions and is not a resolution of this statement.

**Statement check:** Source located. **Status check:** Source report only.
