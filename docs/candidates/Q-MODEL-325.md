Which physical inflationary dynamics, if any, generated the primordial perturbations? For explicit models, relate field content, potential and reheating assumptions to scalar, tensor and non-Gaussian observables capable of distinguishing mechanisms. A reconstructed slow-roll potential under a single-field assumption is not a model-independent identification.
