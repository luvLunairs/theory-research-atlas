For rotating-convection spherical-shell dynamo models, determine whether the strong-field magnetostrophic force balance and associated scaling laws remain valid along physically justified paths toward Earth's very small Ekman and magnetic-Prandtl numbers. Boundary conditions, buoyancy forcing and the asymptotic path must be specified; an MHD limit theorem alone does not identify Earth's actual core regime.
