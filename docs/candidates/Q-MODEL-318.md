Which underlying mass-generation mechanism explains the observed nonzero neutrino masses and mixing? Derive distinguishable consequences of specified Dirac, Majorana or seesaw-type models while retaining their additional fields and assumptions. Fitting oscillation parameters does not uniquely identify the mechanism.
