For every finite contractible two-dimensional polyhedron K, does K×[0,1] admit a triangulation that simplicially collapses to a point?

**Origin:** Literature-origin question. **Curator-designed scope:** No.

**Current status:** Source reports open. **Last checked:** 2026-10-14.

**Status basis:** The 2026 K3 author version retains this question; scoped searches did not establish a general resolution. This is source-reported open status.

**Statement check:** Source located. **Status check:** Source report only.

**Source date and freshness:** Recorded source years: SRC-MATH-303: 2026. The last-checked date records the scope described in the status basis; it is not a comprehensive literature-search date.

**Scope limits:** The statement and locator identify the candidate. A full assumption set and verification protocol remain to be documented.

**Information still needed**

**Primary branch:** [Topology](../domains/math.md#math-top). **Related branches:** [Computational geometry and topology](../domains/cs.md#cs-geom).

**Sources:** [K3: A New Problem List in Low-Dimensional Topology](../sources.md#src-math-303).

**Exact locator:** K3 Problem 5.12; PDF p. 310.

**Potential difficulty:** Frontier. Contractibility is much weaker than collapsibility; the product-with-an-interval conjecture remains a classical challenge.

**Possible AI role:** A tool-using AI could search and independently verify collapse sequences for specified complexes and study which subdivisions make them possible.

**Verification burden:** High. A collapse sequence is locally checkable; lack of a sequence in one triangulation does not prove impossibility in every triangulation.

**Resource expectations:** Finite collapse searches may grow rapidly. No experiments or data are required; the universal statement needs proof.

**Assessment:** 2026-10-14; untested forecast. **Uncertainty:** The candidate permits a suitable triangulation; fixing a triangulation would create a different and potentially false claim.

**Further work:** define a scoped target, resource requirements and a verification plan before developing a full card. **Human review:** Pending.
