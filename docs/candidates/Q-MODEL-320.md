What mechanism explains the exceptionally small effective QCD CP-violating angle while retaining observed quark physics? Compare explicit axion and non-axion mechanisms against radiative stability and empirical constraints. Proving Peccei–Quinn relaxation within its assumed theory does not establish that the theory describes nature.
