# Quadratic generation of matroid toric ideals: the weak White conjecture

For every finite matroid M of positive rank on [n], with set of bases B(M), let phi: Q[y_B : B in B(M)] -> Q[x_1,...,x_n] send y_B to the product of x_i for i in B. Is ker(phi) generated by homogeneous quadratic binomials? All quadratic binomials preserving element multiplicities are allowed; the claim does not require symmetric-exchange generators or a quadratic Gröbner basis.
