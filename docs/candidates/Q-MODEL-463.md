For cosmological solutions of the Einstein equations with compact Cauchy surfaces, under what conditions does at least one Cauchy surface of constant mean curvature exist, and in what sense is its existence generic among the chosen initial data?

**Origin:** Literature-origin question. **Curator-designed scope:** No.

**Current status:** Unverified. **Last checked:** 2026-11-28.

**Status basis:** Galloway and Ling give sufficient conditions using the strong energy condition, an expanding Cauchy surface and future timelike geodesic completeness. Ling and Ohanyan construct vacuum cosmological examples with no CMC Cauchy surface on a class of connected sums. Neither paper classifies generic initial data in the broader program.

**Statement check:** Source located. **Status check:** Later literature checked.

**Source date and freshness:** The 28 November 2026 check covers the works and passages listed in the literature record.

**Scope limits:** Existence of one CMC Cauchy surface is the survey's target. A foliation of the entire spacetime requires additional results. A genericity claim needs a class of initial data and a topology.

**Information still needed**

- Choose the spatial topology, matter assumptions and notion of genericity before comparing existence and nonexistence results.

**Primary branch:** [Gravitation and spacetime theory](../domains/phys.md#phys-grav). **Related branches:** [Integrable and Hamiltonian systems](../domains/mphy.md#mphy-classical).

**Sources:** [Mathematical General Relativity](../sources.md#src-model-405); [A CMC existence result for expanding cosmological spacetimes](../sources.md#src-model-612); [Vacuum cosmological spacetimes without CMC Cauchy surfaces](../sources.md#src-phys-927).

**Resource expectations:** Special cases may permit symbolic analysis or workstation calculations. General nonlinear PDE results require expert proof review, and numerical-relativity exploration may require substantial computing.

**Assessment:** 2026-10-14; untested forecast. **Uncertainty:** Specify global topology, matter and completeness; known no-CMC examples rule out unconditional existence.
