**Possible AI role:** A tool-using AI could search diagrammatic ribbon certificates for specified slice knots and check every band and Reidemeister move independently.

**Resource expectations:** No experiments. Diagram searches may require substantial compute; general obstructions need expert four-manifold methods.

**Assessment:** 2026-10-14; untested forecast. **Uncertainty:** Smooth and topological sliceness differ; handle-ribbon is not automatically ribbon.
