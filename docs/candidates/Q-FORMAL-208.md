Given a complete n-state automaton, nonempty S⊆Q and nonempty A⊊S, with at least one state of A avoidable from Q, determine the tight worst-case length of a word w satisfying A⊈δ(S,w) or |δ(S,w)|<|S|, as a function of n, |S| and |A|, particularly for strongly connected synchronizing automata. The general bound in Lemma 3 is n−|A|.
