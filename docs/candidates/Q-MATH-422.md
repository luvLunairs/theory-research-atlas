If X and Y are independent finitely supported integer-valued random variables and X+Y is uniform on its support, must X and Y each be uniform on their own supports?

**Origin:** Literature-origin question. **Curator-designed scope:** No.

**Current status:** Source reports open. **Last checked:** 2026-11-28.

**Status basis:** Green attributes the question to Emmanuel Amlot. In the monic generating-polynomial formulation, Hare tests candidate coefficient patterns through factor degree 15, with 975 patterns left by that test. Hasanbegović gives a sufficient spacing condition for two-block supports with a fair lower block. Both results allow a general finite support to remain undecided.
