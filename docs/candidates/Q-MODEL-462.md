**Inspected evidence**

- [Mathematical General Relativity](../sources.md#src-model-405) — Section 2.5, paragraph ‘Geodesic hypothesis’, PDF p. 18. Identifies the need for a precise negligible-mass limit when the test body contributes to the spacetime geometry.
- [A Rigorous Derivation of Gravitational Self-force](../sources.md#src-phys-928) — Abstract and corrected version 5 (29 April 2011), analysis of a one-parameter family of metrics. For families satisfying the stated scaling, smoothness and limit conditions, the limiting worldline is a background geodesic. The paper derives first-order self-force and spin-curvature corrections.
- [Equation of Motion of Small Bodies in Relativity](../sources.md#src-phys-929) — Abstract and theorem, assumptions (i)–(iii). A concentration theorem with a dominant energy condition and nearby metrics yields geodesic motion in the small-body limit.
- [Black hole perturbation theory and gravitational self-force](../sources.md#src-phys-930) — Introduction, PDF p. 4. Reviews the small-body expansion and the later extension of gravitational self-force calculations to second perturbative order.

**Limits and remaining work**

- No encompassing existence theorem for arbitrary matter or uniform nonperturbative control of the self-force was established by this search.
