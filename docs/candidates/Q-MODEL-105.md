For the planar circular restricted three-body model with mu=1/100 and unit separation and angular speed, verify conservation of J=2Omega−(x_dot^2+y_dot^2) along smooth collision-free solutions, where Omega=(x^2+y^2)/2+(1−mu)/r_1+mu/r_2, and r_1 and r_2 are distances to the primaries at (−mu,0) and (1−mu,0). At J=16/5, classify the 1/20-by-1/20 closed grid boxes covering [-2,2]^2 as wholly forbidden only if a rigorous upper bound for 2Omega is below J. For every box not containing a primary, report a rigorous finite upper bound for 2Omega and the enclosure method. Mark boxes containing a primary or whose upper bound is not below J as undecided. This is a pointwise exclusion certificate, not an orbit-reachability or topology claim.
