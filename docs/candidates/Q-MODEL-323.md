Determine whether late-time acceleration is explained by a cosmological constant, evolving dark energy or modified gravitational dynamics, using jointly consistent predictions for expansion and structure growth. Any model-specific reconstruction must declare its gravity, matter-coupling and perturbation assumptions; fitting a background expansion curve alone does not establish the mechanism.
