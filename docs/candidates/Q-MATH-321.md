Does an odd positive integer N exist for which σ(N)=2N, where σ sums all positive divisors?

**Origin:** Literature-origin question. **Curator-designed scope:** No.

**Current status:** Source reports open. **Last checked:** 2026-10-14.

**Status basis:** The 2026 research and maintained author page continue to constrain hypothetical odd perfect numbers without exhibiting one or proving nonexistence. No accepted resolution was established in the scoped search; unsolicited proof claims were not treated as established results.

**Assessment:** 2026-10-14; untested forecast. **Uncertainty:** Large lower bounds and excluded exponent patterns do not approach a finite proof of nonexistence by themselves.
