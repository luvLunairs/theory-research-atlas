Must every complete first-order theory in a countable language have either at most countably many or exactly continuum many countable models up to isomorphism, provably in ZFC without assuming the continuum hypothesis?

**Origin:** Literature-origin question. **Curator-designed scope:** No.

**Current status:** Source reports open. **Last checked:** 2026-11-26.

**Status basis:** The June 2026 study of Scott ranks describes the first-order conjecture as open. Kurilić's September preprint proves the dichotomy for the complete theories of a class of partially ordered sets built from rooted trees. The unrestricted first-order question remains open in the cited literature.

**Statement check:** Source located. **Status check:** Later literature checked.

**Source date and freshness:** The June 2026 Scott-analysis paper predates Kurilić's September 2026 preprint.

**Scope limits:** The lexicographic sum is indexed by a finite partial order. Each summand is a finite direct product of rooted trees. The trees themselves may be infinite.

**Information still needed**

- A general resolution or a counterexample outside the classes covered by existing theorems.

**Primary branch:** [Model theory](../domains/logic.md#logic-model). **Related branches:** [Set theory and independence](../domains/logic.md#logic-set).

**Sources:** [Scott Analysis below the Vaught Ordinal](../sources.md#src-formal-314); [Vaught's Conjecture for Sums of Products of Rooted Trees](../sources.md#src-math-910).

**Exact locator:** Scott Analysis below the Vaught Ordinal, introduction, and Kurilić, Theorem 4.1(a)

**Potential difficulty:** Frontier. It requires global control of countable-model isomorphism types, not enumeration at fixed finite size.

**Possible AI role:** Audit Scott-rank arguments and test whether a proposed restricted theory really satisfies its definability hypotheses.

**Verification burden:** High. A proof must preserve countability and isomorphism conventions and avoid inadvertently assuming CH.

**Resource expectations:** No physical experiment or external dataset is required. Main cost is specialist proof development and review; finite searches can grow exponentially and need a separately specified limit.

**Assessment:** 2026-10-14; untested forecast. **Uncertainty:** No named model has been evaluated. These are possible research-support roles, not evidence that current AI can resolve the general problem.

**Further work:** define a scoped target, resource requirements and a verification plan before developing a full card. **Human review:** Pending.
