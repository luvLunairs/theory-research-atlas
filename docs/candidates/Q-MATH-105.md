Reproduce the eigenvalues (n*pi)^2 and normalized eigenfunctions sqrt(2) sin(n*pi*x), n >= 1, for -u'' on [0,1] with u(0)=u(1)=0. Prove their completeness in L^2([0,1]) and state separately sufficient regularity and the Dirichlet boundary compatibility f(0)=f(1)=0 for uniform convergence on [0,1]; finite numerical eigenvalue agreement is insufficient.
