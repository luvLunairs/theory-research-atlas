For a specified disordered interacting spin-chain Hamiltonian, determine whether thermal-inclusion avalanche dynamics accounts for its putative ETH–MBL transition, and derive a falsifiable quantitative consequence from the microscopic model. The question is conditional on the existence of the relevant transition; a phenomenological avalanche rule alone is not its derivation.
