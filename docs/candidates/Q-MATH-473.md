Is the standard restricted wreath product of two finitely generated Hopfian groups necessarily Hopfian? A group is Hopfian when every surjective endomorphism is injective.

### 2026-11-28

**Coverage:** Statement and later literature. **Statement:** Matched.

**Recorded status:** Source reports open → Unverified.

Kriebel gives a negative answer conditional on a characteristic-two group-algebra construction: both the two-element base group and the finitely generated acting group are Hopfian, but their restricted wreath product is claimed to admit a surjective endomorphism with nontrivial kernel. Jaikin-Zapirain subsequently describes the upstream construction. Independent confirmation of the complete wreath-product argument has not been established here.

**Search scope:** Kourovka 21.22. Bradford and Fournier-Facio, abelian-base characterization. Kriebel, section on Hopfian factors.

**Inspected evidence**

- [Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook (21st edition, November 2026 update)](../sources.md#src-math-900) — Problem 21.22. The original question asks about the standard restricted product of two finitely generated Hopfian groups.
- [Hopfian wreath products and the stable finiteness conjecture](../sources.md#src-math-922) — Repository abstract, characterization for a finitely generated abelian base and a finitely generated Hopfian acting group. Relates Hopficity for all wreath products in the abelian-base case to Kaplansky’s stable finiteness conjecture.
- [Two consequences of group-algebra and planar Fourier certificates: wreath products and triangle bounds](../sources.md#src-math-923) — 6 October, version 1.0.0, sections on Hopfian factors and planar packing. Derives a group with Hopfian factors and a non-Hopfian wreath product from an external group-algebra theorem, and derives a planar bound from a separate Fourier certificate.
- [A Torsion-Free Group Algebra That Is Not Directly Finite](../sources.md#src-math-924) — 4 October release, build/sections/introduction.tex, Theorem 1.1. Reports a finitely presented torsion-free group whose group algebra over the field of two elements contains a one-sided inverse that is not two-sided.
- [A Note on the OpenAI Construction of Counterexamples to the Kaplansky Direct-Finiteness Conjecture](../sources.md#src-math-925) — 5 November, abstract. Presents a subsequent exposition of the characteristic-two construction and an adaptation to hyperbolic groups in each positive characteristic.
