# Approximate equilibrium in quitting games with at least four players

Does every finite-player quitting game with at least four players admit an epsilon-Nash equilibrium for every epsilon>0? Each player independently randomizes a quitting time in N∪{infinity}; bounded payoffs depend on the first quitting set, with a specified payoff if nobody quits. External public correlation is not allowed.

**Origin:** Literature-origin question. **Curator-designed scope:** No.

**Current status:** Source reports open. **Last checked:** 2026-11-28.

**Status basis:** The November stopping-game paper gives ε-equilibria with arbitrarily many players under a conditionally atomless filtration, and for three players with any filtration. The ordinary four-or-more-player quitting game allows no extra public randomness. The article leaves unrestricted stopping games with more than three players open.
