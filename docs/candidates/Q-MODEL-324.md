Determine whether the discrepancy among early-universe-inferred and late-universe Hubble expansion estimates is explained by observational systematics, modeling assumptions or new physics. A proposed explanation must be assessed jointly against the relevant calibration, CMB, BAO and distance data, with correlations and model dependence explicit.
