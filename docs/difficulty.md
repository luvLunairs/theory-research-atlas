# Browse by difficulty

[Browse](README.md) · [Results](results.md) · [Contribute](../CONTRIBUTING.md)

Difficulty concerns the stated target, not the ease of stating a question. A finite instance can be difficult to discover and easy to check. A high or frontier rating does not rule out useful partial progress. These are dated, untested editorial forecasts; no model success rate is implied. See the [assessment rubric](ai-assessment.md) for definitions.

| Rating | Meaning | Open-status evidence | Provisional or conflicting status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Low | A routine or tightly bounded task under stated prerequisites. | 0 | 0 |
| Medium | Substantial but structured work; feasibility still needs testing. | 0 | 0 |
| High | Specialist research with significant unresolved obstacles. | 62 | 99 |
| Frontier | A major long-standing target or comparably deep general problem. | 77 | 17 |
