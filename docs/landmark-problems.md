# Millennium and Hilbert problems

[Browse](README.md) · [Results](results.md) · [Contribute](../CONTRIBUTING.md)

These historical collections provide a map of influential research directions. Their entries are not all unsolved, and a broad research program may have no single yes-or-no resolution. Collection entries are not added to the candidate count; links reuse canonical problem records. Unlinked open components still need a precise statement and specialist assessment.

A reported resolution awaiting scrutiny is kept distinct from an accepted solution. For the Millennium collection, numbering below is a navigation convention, not an official problem number.

<summary>1. Continuum hypothesis</summary>

Does 2^aleph_0=aleph_1? Hilbert also asked whether the real numbers can be well ordered; that component follows from the axiom of choice in ZFC.

**Status:** Independence. The continuum hypothesis is independent of ZFC, assuming its consistency. The well-ordering component is a theorem of ZFC; the two claims must not share an undifferentiated open label.

**Sources:** [Mathematical Problems](sources.md#src-land-307); [Solved, Unsolved and Unsolvable: The Status of Hilbert's 23 Problems in Mathematics](sources.md#src-land-308).

**Locator:** Hilbert, Problem 1; recent overview, matching numbered paragraph. Additional scope qualifications use the linked specialist source.

**Checked:** 2026-10-14.

</details>
